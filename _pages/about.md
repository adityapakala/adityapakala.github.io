Hi, I'm **Aditya Pakala**. I work at the intersection of AI research and software engineering, with a focus on building and evaluating intelligent systems that work in practice.

At Amazon, my work includes multi-agent systems, grounding, and LLM evaluation. Previously, I've worked on applied AI at Walmart Global Tech, machine learning infrastructure at ZeoAuto, and multimodal research at the AI Institute, University of South Carolina.

I'm interested in making AI systems more capable, reliable, and useful.

## Research interests

Large language models and agents, evaluation and reliability, multimodal learning, and production machine learning systems.

## Selected work

- **Agent reliability and evaluation:** Grounding guardrails, multi-judge evaluation, and systematic testing of agent diagnoses.
- **Applied AI systems:** Retrieval, data pipelines, and scalable ML inference in production.
- **Multimodal research:** FACTIFY3M, a benchmark for multimodal fact verification, published at EMNLP 2023.
