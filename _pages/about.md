Hi, I'm **Aditya Pakala**. I'm an engineer working across **AI research, machine learning, backend systems, and production software**.

My path has taken me from academic research to a Y Combinator startup, the world's largest retailer, and teams at Amazon. I like understanding problems deeply, building systems that work, and learning across different domains.

## My journey

| Where | What I worked on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IIT Delhi** · Research | Machine learning for predictive maintenance, equipment failure prediction, and explainability. |
| **AI Institute, University of South Carolina** · Research | Multimodal fact verification and evaluation, including **FACTIFY3M**, published at **EMNLP 2023**. |
| **ZeoAuto (Y Combinator)** · Startup | Backend engineering, ML-powered routing, and production inference services. |
| **Walmart Global Tech** · Fortune 1 | Applied AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and large-scale retail data systems. |
| **Amazon Robotics** · Big tech | Software and AI systems for real-world robotics operations, reliability, and evaluation. |
| **Amazon Ads** · Big tech | My latest chapter, bringing my research and engineering background to advertising technology. |

This progression has given me experience with different kinds of problems: **research questions, startup constraints, large-scale data, physical-world systems, and customer-facing technology**.

## What I focus on

- **AI and research:** LLMs, agents, multimodal learning, model evaluation, and explainability.
- **Software engineering:** Backend services, APIs, cloud infrastructure, and reliable production systems.
- **Applied machine learning:** Turning models and data into useful tools with measurable outcomes.
